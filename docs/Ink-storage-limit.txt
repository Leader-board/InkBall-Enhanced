Configurable ink storage — 21 November 2026

The default capacity now matches the original InkBall: 20 strokes.
Game > Board Mechanics > Ink storage lets you choose 1 to 4096.
Restore Original Defaults fills in 20; Apply or OK applies it, Cancel
leaves the current setting intact. The preference is saved across launches
and retained across boards, restarts and new games.

When full, drawing a new stroke removes the oldest stored stroke. Lowering
the limit immediately removes the oldest excess strokes. Removed strokes
no longer appear or collide with balls. Total ink-drawn statistics are
preserved, since they measure all ink drawn, including removed ink.
The existing 32,768-point per-stroke limit remains unchanged.
