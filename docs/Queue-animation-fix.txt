Queue animation fix — 14 November 2026

Corrected the cosmetic queue animation in the upper-left black strip.
The head uses acceleration increment 6; following slots use 5,4,3,2,1,
matching the installed official EXE's UpdateBallPositions routine. The
reconstruction had incorrectly used 1,2,3,4,5 for following slots.

The visual integration step is now the original nominal 15 ms rather than
1/60 second. Elapsed simulation time is accumulated so coalesced paints
still catch up, and paused/repeated redraws do not advance the animation.
Actual scheduling of the legacy Windows executable can vary; this restores
its nominal cadence and verified queue-motion arithmetic, without claiming
frame-for-frame identity under every operating-system scheduling condition.

No on-board ball velocity, random speed selection, launch countdown, physics,
statistics, or settings were changed.
